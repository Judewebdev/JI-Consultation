How the work runs

Diagnose, build, compound.

Same three moves in both arms. The order matters more than the effort, which is why most people who work hard still stall.

01

Diagnose

We start with a conversation, not a proposal. Half the time the thing you came to fix is a symptom of something one layer down. We would rather find that first than sell you the wrong solution well.

02

Build

Positioning, then systems, then skills, in that order. Building a content engine on top of unclear positioning just helps more people misunderstand you faster.

03

Compound

The goal is not a good month. It is a structure that keeps producing after the engagement ends, and a person who can run it without you.

Before you book

Straight answers

Agency or academy, which one is for me?

If you have a business or a role that already generates something and you need it positioned and systemised, that is the agency. If you need the underlying skill before any of that is even relevant, that is the academy. If you are not sure, book the call and say so, we will tell you honestly even when the honest answer is the cheaper one.

Do you work with clients outside Nigeria?

Yes. I have handled media and website marketing for a United States nonprofit and social media for a Delaware based brand. The work runs remotely by default, and time zones have never been the hard part.

What does it cost?

It depends on scope, and any consultant who quotes you before understanding the problem is selling a package rather than a solution. Bring your situation to the call and you will leave with a number and a clear reason for it.

Do you take one off projects?

Sometimes, but be aware of what you are buying. A one off deliverable without the positioning behind it tends to look good and do very little. If that is genuinely all you need, say so and we will scope it properly.

How is the faith side handled in commercial work?

Directly but not imposed. My conviction is that faith and excellence belong in the same room, and it shapes how I work: honest scoping, no manipulation tactics, no selling people what they do not need. Client work is judged on results, not on what you believe.

How quickly can we start?

Discovery calls usually happen within the week. Agency engagements start once scope is agreed. Academy programmes run in cohorts, so the start date depends on where the current intake sits. Ask on the call and you will get the real date.
