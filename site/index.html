The real problem

Most businesses are not underperforming. They are unreadable.

The service is solid. The team delivers. And still the pipeline depends on referrals and luck, because nothing outside the business explains clearly what it does, who it is for, or why it is worth the money. That gap is not a marketing budget problem. It is a systems problem, and it is fixable.

01

You are the bottleneck

Every deal needs you in the room. Nothing moves when you are busy, sick or asleep, so growth is capped at your calendar.

02

Marketing is a scramble

Posting when there is time, going quiet when there is not. No compounding, because nothing runs on a system.

03

You cannot forecast

You know what came in last month. You have no idea what is coming next month, which makes every decision a gamble.

Why we exist

The gap is rarely quality.

Almost every business we meet is better than its reputation suggests. The work is good. The clients who find them stay. But finding them is the problem, and the usual answer, hire an agency to post more, treats the symptom.

We work the other way round. Positioning first, so the market can place you in seconds. Then the offer and the pricing, so the value is legible. Then the site, the content, the campaigns and the automations, so the whole thing runs without you having to be in every room.

That sequence is not a preference. Run it out of order and you spend money teaching more people to misunderstand what you sell.

We scope in writing

Price, deliverables and dates agreed before work starts. No open-ended retainers that quietly become a subscription.

We say no

If the thing you asked for will not fix the thing you described, we say so on the call. Selling the wrong solution well is still selling the wrong solution.

We hand over

Every engagement ends with your team able to run what we built. Dependency is a business model, not a service.

The team

Two senior people on every account.

You talk to the people doing the work. There is no account manager relaying messages to someone you never meet.

Before you ask

Straight answers.

What does it cost?

It depends on scope, and any consultant who quotes before understanding the problem is selling a package rather than a solution. Bring your situation to the discovery call and you leave with a number and the reasoning behind it.

Can we start with one service instead of everything?

Yes, and often you should. But be aware of what you are buying. A new site on top of unclear positioning looks better and converts about the same. If we think the order is wrong, we will tell you before you spend.

Do you work with clients outside Nigeria?

Yes. The team spans Nigeria and Canada, and we have delivered media and website work for organisations in the United States. Everything runs remotely by default.

Who actually does the work?

The senior team you meet on the call. Specialists come in for production work like editing and paid media, but the strategy and the account lead do not change hands.

How quickly can we start?

Discovery calls usually happen within the week. Once scope is signed, project work typically starts within ten working days. If we cannot hit a date you need, we say so rather than promising it.

What if we want to bring it in house later?

Good. That is the intended ending. Everything is documented as we build, and we train your team on handover. You own the accounts, the assets and the access from day one.

Tell us what is actually stuck.

No deck required. Describe the situation in plain words and we will tell you whether we are the right people for it.

Questions

What does a programme cost?

Do I need experience to start?

About the academy

Skills are the one asset nobody can repossess.

JI Global Academy is the teaching arm of the consultancy. It exists because the fastest way to change someone's income is to give them a skill the market is already paying for, taught by people who are being paid for it right now.

The thinking

Access is the bottleneck, not ability.

There is no shortage of capable people. There is a shortage of people who ever got shown what the work actually looks like, at the standard it has to meet, by someone doing it commercially.

That is the entire gap the academy closes. We are not trying to be a university. We are trying to get someone from not knowing to being paid, in the shortest honest distance, and then keep the standard high enough that being paid continues.

The agency funds it and feeds it. Client work sets the curriculum, and the strongest graduates get referred into that same client work. The two arms are not a marketing structure, they are the same loop.

Taught by practitioners

Everyone teaching is delivering the same discipline to paying clients. Nobody here teaches full time.

Honest about outcomes

We do not promise jobs, incomes or timelines we cannot control. What we control is the standard of the teaching and the feedback.

Character alongside skill

Capability without judgement is a liability. The mentorship track exists because skills that arrive faster than character tend not to last.
